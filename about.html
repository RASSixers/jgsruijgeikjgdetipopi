About SixersHoops

Independent Philadelphia 76ers coverage, tools, and community

Who we are: SixersHoops is an independent fan site for Philadelphia 76ers news, analysis, Pick'em, and interactive tools. We are not affiliated with the NBA, the Philadelphia 76ers, or Harris Blitzer Sports & Entertainment.

Our mission

We built SixersHoops to give fans a single place for clear reporting, practical tools, and community features. Whether you follow every game or check in a few times a week, the goal is the same: accurate information, useful products, and a respectful space for Sixers fans.

What you will find here

  • News and analysis from the staff desk, including player grades, previews, and recaps when published.
  • Pick'em daily prediction games with leaderboards and friend groups.
  • Team tools such as roster, depth chart, salary sheet, future draft picks, and schedule with live score support.
  • Trade Machine and related roster tools for exploring possible moves.
  • From the Desk social updates and community features for fans on the site.

How we work

Accuracy comes first. We aim to verify information before it goes live and to correct mistakes when we find them. Analysis and opinions are labeled as such. Tools such as salaries, picks, and trade data are updated as sources allow; always treat them as fan resources, not official team documents.

Independence

SixersHoops is fan-operated. Team names, logos, and trademarks belong to their respective owners and appear here for editorial and informational use only. Nothing on this site is official communication from the Sixers or the NBA.
