Sixers Player Grades: Sixers Fend Off Pacers Comeback, 114-109

Sixers Hoops Staff·October 28, 2026

Player Grades

B+

Joel Embiid

39 min, 21 pts (9-17 FG, 0-3 3P, 3-3 FT), 11 reb, 2 ast, 4 TO, 1 blk, -4

He scored 14 in the first half, then went scoreless for a long stretch after halftime. The offense stalled whenever the ball stuck in his hands, and the jumpers late helped.

A-

Tyrese Maxey

36 min, 27 pts (8-18 FG, 3-7 3P, 8-9 FT), 5 reb, 4 ast, 4 TO, 1 stl, +6

He was invisible early, with no points in the opening stretch. By the fourth he was the one the offense needed, and his finish through contact and a late bucket put the Pacers away. The rare turnovers were the only thing dragging at his night

B+

Jaylen Brown

34 min, 20 pts (7-13 FG, 1-3 3P, 5-5 FT), 10 reb, 2 ast, 5 TO, 1 stl, 1 blk, +3

He made the most of his touches, hitting every free throw and creating extra possessions by crashing the offensive glass. Five turnovers gave some of that back.

B+

LeBron James

33 min, 13 pts (5-12 FG, 2-3 3P, 1-2 FT), 5 reb, 10 ast, 0 TO, 1 stl, 2 blk, +3

He initiated most of the half-court offense without a single turnover, and his fourth-quarter pass to Simons is the clearest sign of how well he read Indiana’s defense did have a few rough fouls in the 4th.

B

VJ Edgecombe

29 min, 8 pts (3-10 FG, 1-6 3P, 1-1 FT), 7 reb, 2 ast, 3 TO, 1 stl, -5

In his first game since the abdominal bruise, he was active on the glass but shot poorly from three and made a few rushed decisions with the ball, did have a great defensive play in the 4th that may have swung the game in the sixers favor.

A

Anfernee Simons

27 min, 20 pts (7-11 FG, 6-10 3P, 0-0 FT), 3 reb, 1 ast, 0 TO, 1 stl, +14

He entered the game cold from the season’s first few outings, and scored 12 points in about eight and a half minutes off the bench. He picked his spots, shot in rhythm, and never had to force one.

C+

Kentavious Caldwell-Pope

21 min, 4 pts (1-4 FG, 0-3 3P, 2-2 FT), 2 reb, 1 ast, 0 TO, 2 stl, +7

His shot never fell, but he made himself useful with active hands on defense.

D+

Dean Wade

17 min, 0 pts (0-1 FG, 0-1 3P, 0-0 FT), 1 reb, 1 ast, 0 TO, +1

He had almost no offensive output, and five fouls in a short stint kept him from affecting the game.

INC

Adem Bona

4 min, 1 pts (0-1 FG, 0-0 3P, 1-2 FT), 1 reb, 0 ast, 0 TO, 1 blk, 0

He played only a few minutes, mostly late, and didn’t change anything either way.
