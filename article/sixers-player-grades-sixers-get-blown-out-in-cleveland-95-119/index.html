Sixers Hoops Staff·November 6, 2026

Player Grades

B+

Jaylen Brown

34 min, 20 pts (9-19 FG, 2-9 3P, 0-0 FT), 5 reb, 5 ast, 2 TO, 1 stl, -22

Tried to will the team back into it with aggression and playmaking. Scored in a variety of ways and kept finding open teammates even when the shot was not falling consistently. The effort was there even if the final result was not.

D+

LeBron James

32 min, 8 pts (3-12 FG, 0-4 3P, 2-2 FT), 7 reb, 3 ast, 6 TO, 2 stl, 1 blk, -8

The return to Cleveland clearly did not go the way anyone hoped. The legs looked heavy, the shot was not there, and the turnovers piled up. Still competed on the glass but the overall impact was well below his standard.

C+

Tyrese Maxey

32 min, 17 pts (5-15 FG, 2-9 3P, 5-6 FT), 3 reb, 4 ast, 0 TO, 1 stl, -19

Fought through a tough shooting night and still found ways to get to the free throw line and create for others. The usual burst was missing at times but he never stopped pushing.

A-

VJ Edgecombe

32 min, 19 pts (8-13 FG, 3-5 3P, 0-0 FT), 3 reb, 2 ast, 0 TO, 4 stl, -11

He was the one Sixer who actually looked engaged the entire night. Attacked closeouts, finished through contact, and racked up steals that created a few transition chances. On a night when almost everyone else looked flat he still competed.

D

Joel Embiid

25 min, 6 pts (0-9 FG, 0-2 3P, 6-6 FT), 7 reb, 1 ast, 2 TO, 2 blk, -15

Completely out of rhythm from the jump. Could not get anything to fall and the usual physical presence was missing. Free throws kept the line a little cleaner but the night was a struggle.
