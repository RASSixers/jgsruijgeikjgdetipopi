VJ Edgecombe

43 min, 24 pts (8-15 FG, 4-9 3P, 4-4 FT), 8 reb, 6 ast, 4 TO, 2 stl, +7

Season-high 24 points with eight rebounds and six assists in heavy minutes. Hit four threes and made the biggest one of the night on a no-look pass from LeBron that put them ahead late. He handled the extra creation load with Maxey and Brown out and never forced the issue. A-

Anfernee Simons

38 min, 18 pts (7-12 FG, 3-7 3P, 1-1 FT), 2 reb, 2 ast, 0 TO, +9

36 min, 26 pts (7-18 FG, 0-1 3P, 12-14 FT), 6 reb, 7 ast, 3 TO, 2 stl, 3 blk, -1
