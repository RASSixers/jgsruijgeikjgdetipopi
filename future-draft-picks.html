Asset Intel
The 2028 1st — Boston Swap Rights
Boston holds swap rights on the better of Philadelphia's own and the LA Clippers' 2028 first-round picks. If Philly's own pick lands top-8 or the Clippers' pick lands top-16, Boston can swap in its own 2028 first (net of existing Spurs protections) for the better of the two. Otherwise, Boston simply keeps the Clippers' first outright and Philadelphia retains its own pick.
The 2029 Swap
The 2029 1st-round pick includes "Swap Best" rights with the LA Clippers, Top-3 Protected. The Sixers take the better of the two picks unless the Clippers land in the Top 3.
The 2028 PHI 2nd — Encumbered
Philadelphia's own 2028 2nd is ineligible to be traded (encumbered to Brooklyn from the James Harden trade). It conveys but cannot be moved.
The 2028 DET 2nd — Fake Asset
The Detroit 2nd is 31-55 protected, meaning it only conveys as picks 56-60. With only 5 slots to convey, it is a near-certain non-conveyor and a functionally fake asset.
The 2031 1st — Sent to Boston
Philadelphia's own 2031 first-rounder was traded to Boston outright, with no protections remaining.
