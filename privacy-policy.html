Last updated: October 14, 2026

Summary: We collect limited information needed to run accounts, features, and the Site. We do not sell your personal information. Contact us at info@sixershoops.com with privacy questions.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how SixersHoops ("we," "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Site. It applies to visitors and registered users.

2. Information we collect

Account information: If you sign in (for example with Google or email), we may receive your name, email address, profile photo, and a unique account identifier from the authentication provider.

Profile information: Username, display name, avatar, and similar details you choose to provide.

User Content: Content you submit, including posts, comments, chat messages, trade ideas, Pick'em picks, and contact form messages.

Usage and device data: Log data such as browser type, approximate location derived from IP, pages viewed, and timestamps. We may use analytics tools that set cookies or similar technologies.

Communications: Messages you send us through the contact form or email.

3. How we use information

  • To operate, secure, and improve the Site and its features
  • To create and manage accounts and show public profile information you choose to share
  • To display User Content you submit and to moderate the community
  • To respond to contact requests and support issues
  • To enforce our Terms, prevent abuse, and protect rights and safety
  • To comply with law and respond to lawful requests

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with:

  • Service providers that host data, authenticate users, or provide analytics (for example cloud and auth providers), under obligations to protect data
  • Other users, when you post public or group-visible User Content (including username and profile details you make visible)
  • Legal and safety recipients when required by law or to protect rights, safety, or the Site
  • Business transfers, if we transfer operations, subject to appropriate protections

5. Public and community content

Content you post in public or group areas (including chat, comments, and trade posts) may be visible to other users. Do not post sensitive personal data you do not want others to see. We are not responsible for how other users use information you make available.

6. Cookies and similar technologies

We use cookies and local storage for sign-in sessions, preferences (such as theme), and analytics. See our Cookie Policy for more detail.

7. Data retention

We retain account and content data as long as needed to operate the Site and for legitimate business or legal purposes. You may request deletion of your account by contacting us; some information may remain in backups or logs for a limited period, or where we must keep it for legal reasons.

8. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. You use the Site at your own risk.

9. Children

The Site is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your region). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

10. Your choices

  • Update profile information in account settings where available
  • Control some cookies through your browser settings
  • Request access, correction, or deletion by emailing info@sixershoops.com

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under applicable privacy laws. We will respond to verifiable requests as required by law.
