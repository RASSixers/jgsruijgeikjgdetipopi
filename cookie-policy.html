Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Overview: SixersHoops uses cookies and similar technologies to keep you signed in, remember preferences, and understand how the Site is used.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. Local storage and similar technologies can serve related purposes. This policy covers those technologies together as "cookies."

2. How we use cookies

  • Essential: Sign-in session, security, and load balancing needed for the Site to work
  • Preferences: Theme (light or dark) and similar settings you choose
  • Analytics: Aggregated usage data (for example page views) to improve the Site

3. Third parties

Embedded content (such as social media posts) and authentication providers may set their own cookies. Those parties control their cookies under their own policies. We do not control third-party cookies.

4. Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking essential cookies may prevent sign-in or other features from working. Preference cookies can usually be cleared by resetting Site settings or clearing site data.

5. More information

For how we handle personal information more broadly, see our Privacy Policy. Questions: info@sixershoops.com.
