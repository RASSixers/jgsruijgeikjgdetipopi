Player Grades

B-

Tyrese Maxey

35 min, 22 pts (7-16 FG, 2-7 3P, 6-6 FT), 2 reb, 5 ast, 3 TO, -23

22 points and five assists. Got to the free throw line every time he attacked and kept the ball moving. The three-point shooting was off and the defensive pressure on Portland’s guards faded in the second half.

B

VJ Edgecombe

35 min, 12 pts (5-11 FG, 1-4 3P, 1-1 FT), 7 reb, 7 ast, 3 TO, 2 stl, -2

12 points, seven rebounds, and seven assists. Filled the stat sheet and stayed active on both ends for 35 minutes. One of the few who kept competing when the third quarter turned into a three-point barrage.

A

LeBron James

30 min, 23 pts (8-13 FG, 4-5 3P, 3-4 FT), 6 reb, 3 ast, 1 TO, -13

Efficient 23 points on 8-of-13 shooting with four threes. Carried the early offense and kept finding ways to score even as Portland pulled away. The rebounding and playmaking were solid but the supporting cast could not match the Blazers’ three-point volume.

A-

Jaylen Brown

29 min, 20 pts (8-14 FG, 1-3 3P, 3-4 FT), 2 reb, 4 ast, 0 TO, 2 stl, -11

20 points in his return. Attacked the paint, finished through contact, and added two steals. Looked healthy and aggressive but the team defense around him collapsed once the Blazers started knocking down threes.

10 points and five rebounds in 20 minutes at center. Fought on the glass and got to the line but struggled to protect the paint against Portland’s size and spacing.
