Sixers Player Grades: Sixers Hold Off Knicks Comeback, 98-93

Sixers Hoops Staff·October 30, 2026

Player Grades

A+

Tyrese Maxey

38 min, 27 pts (10-19 FG, 3-8 3P, 4-7 FT), 4 reb, 4 ast, 2 TO, 1 stl, 1 blk, -1

His defense mattered as much as his scoring early. He blocked a pull-up from Brunson before it left his hands, which helped throw New York’s offense off. He sealed the win at the line in the final minute

A-

VJ Edgecombe

36 min, 13 pts (6-10 FG, 1-4 3P, 0-0 FT), 8 reb, 2 ast, 2 TO, +4

He was efficient with his shots, active on the glass, and kept the offense moving. He gave the starting unit a steady third scorer without forcing anything. Clutch 3 to seal the game at the end of the 4th.

A

LeBron James

34 min, 20 pts (8-15 FG, 2-6 3P, 2-3 FT), 9 reb, 8 ast, 2 TO, 1 stl, 1 blk, +8

This was his best game of the season. He ran the offense, rebounded, and took over the scoring when Philly needed it. Down the stretch, he was the one the team trusted with the ball.

B-

Joel Embiid

33 min, 10 pts (3-12 FG, 0-4 3P, 4-4 FT), 7 reb, 7 ast, 4 TO, 1 stl, 1 blk, +8

The shot wasn’t falling, and he gave the ball away too often, but he was a good passer out of double-teams and hit his free throws. He was also stuck guarding Towns for stretches, which took a toll.

B-

Dean Wade

27 min, 0 pts (0-0 FG, 0-0 3P, 0-0 FT), 2 reb, 1 ast, 0 TO, 3 stl, -6

He got the start in Brown’s place. He didn’t take a shot, but he was active defensively and came up with several steals

A+

Anfernee Simons

31 min, 21 pts (7-11 FG, 5-8 3P, 2-2 FT), 2 reb, 1 ast, 1 TO, +6

He came off the bench hot and shot Philly into the lead. The shooting kept the floor spaced for LeBron and Embiid to work.

B+

Kentavious Caldwell-Pope
